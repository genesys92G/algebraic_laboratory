Algebraic Laboratory

Basic Operations

Explore algebra with your hands. Build expressions with algebra tiles, combine and cancel terms to add and subtract, factor, combine like terms, multiply with the distributive property or a grid, and group everything with nested parentheses, brackets and braces.

How to use it

Instructions

Tiles

  • Add tiles from the right panel (or create custom variables and numbers). Drag to move; drag several at once with a selection box or Ctrl+E (select all).
  • Drop a tile on a like tile to add them; drop it on its opposite to cancel. Right-click changes the sign; double-click edits exponent, coefficient or value; hold a tile for one second to split or gather it.
  • C swaps the positions of two selected tiles. O orders the selected tiles from the highest to the lowest degree (then by letter) and centers them in the work area, in one line or several.
  • Ctrl+C / Ctrl+V copy and paste, Ctrl+Z undo, Ctrl+X redo, Delete removes the selection.

Containers and nested groupings

  • Click a container header to select it (click again to deselect); Delete or the trash icon removes it with everything inside.
  • The gear menu offers Apply Signs, Separator, the grouping symbol — ( ) parentheses, [ ] brackets or { } braces — Add nested group, Select and Delete.
  • Drag a container inside another one to nest it, or drag it out to un-nest it. Nested groupings are shown in the expression display.
  • Type expressions such as 2x - [3 - (y + 1)] (addition mode) or 2{x + [y - (z + 1)]} and (a+2b)(3x-y) (multiplication mode).

Factoring (Addition mode)

  • Select a sum — two or more tiles (drag a selection box) or a container (click its header) — and press the factor button (amber logo, top-right) or F. The lab first tells you whether the sum is factorable.
  • It follows the general strategy: 1) common factor (numbers, then variables with the lowest exponent; the minus sign too if the first term is negative); 2) count the terms: two terms → difference of squares, sum or difference of cubes; three terms → perfect square trinomial, x²+bx+c or ax²+bx+c (ac method); four terms → grouping, perfect cube, or a trinomial square minus a square; 3) keep factoring every new factor until it is prime; 4) multiply back to check.
  • Every step explains what to look for and what to do, with a card and a live expression. Use the step controls (automatic, manual, tile interaction, skip) to go at your own pace. If nothing applies, it explains why the sum is not factorable.

Like terms (T) and elements (E)

  • Select several terms and press T (Addition and Multiplication modes). The lab marks which terms are alike and which are not, then merges the like ones tile by tile, showing how the coefficients add or subtract, and finally orders the result.
  • After a multiplication (distributive property or grid) the like terms of the result are also combined slowly, tile by tile.
  • Select terms and press E to see the elements of the expression centred on the screen: sign, coefficient, variable, exponent, constant term, degree, and a summary. Click anywhere, press Esc or use the ✕ to close it.

Multiplication and the distributive property

  • Switch to × mode. Place an outside factor next to a container and click it, or double-click a container header to multiply it by the nearest container.
  • Step controls (icons only): automatic, manual (next step), tile interaction on/off (copies of the factors fly down and merge into the new term), skip to the result.

Grid multiplication

  • In × mode, press the table button in the top-left corner. It works with a parenthesis and a number or variable outside it (for example 3x(y+2)), with two or more parentheses, and with negative signs. If you select factors first (click a header, or drag a selection box around tiles) only those are used; otherwise everything on the board is multiplied.
  • The factor with more terms goes across the top and the other down the side. With three or more factors the grid is built in stages, one factor at a time. Use the same step controls to fill the cells; then like terms fly into the result area and merge pair by pair, and the result appears as tiles ordered by degree. Press ✕ to leave the grid and send the result to the work area.

More

  • The violet logo (top-right) generates an exercise; hold it to configure. The amber logo below it factors a selected sum. ± Sign Rules shows the sign reference. Click the sidebar title to return to the start screen.
