Basic Operations

How to use it

Instructions

Tiles

  • Add tiles from the right panel (or create custom variables and numbers). Drag to move; drag several at once with a selection box or Ctrl+E (select all).
  • Drop a tile on a like tile to add them; drop it on its opposite to cancel. Right-click changes the sign; double-click edits exponent, coefficient or value; hold a tile for one second to split or gather it.
  • C swaps the positions of two selected tiles. O orders the selected tiles from the highest to the lowest degree (then by letter) and centers them in the work area, in one line or several.
  • Ctrl+C / Ctrl+V copy and paste, Ctrl+Z undo, Ctrl+X redo, Delete removes the selection.

Containers and nested groupings

  • Click a container header to select it (click again to deselect); Delete or the trash icon removes it with everything inside.
  • The gear menu offers Apply Signs, Separator, the grouping symbol — ( ) parentheses, [ ] brackets or { } braces — Add nested group, Select and Delete.
  • Drag a container inside another one to nest it, or drag it out to un-nest it. Nested groupings are shown in the expression display.
  • Type expressions such as 2x - [3 - (y + 1)] (addition mode) or 2{x + [y - (z + 1)]} and (a+2b)(3x-y) (multiplication mode).

Multiplication and the distributive property

  • Switch to × mode. Place an outside factor next to a container and click it, or double-click a container header to multiply it by the nearest container.
  • Step controls (icons only): automatic, manual (next step), tile interaction on/off (copies of the factors fly down and merge into the new term), skip to the result.

Grid multiplication

  • In × mode, select two containers (click their headers) and press the table button in the top-left corner. Their terms move into a grid: the first container across the top, the second down the side.
  • Use the same step controls to fill the cells. The left panel lists every action; at the end like terms are combined and the result appears below as tiles ordered by degree and letter. Press ✕ to leave the grid and send the result to the work area.

More

  • The violet logo (top-right) generates an exercise; hold it to configure. ± Sign Rules shows the sign reference. Click the sidebar title to return to the start screen.
